--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Dietary assessment: 24-hour recall (multiple pass), Food Frequency Questionnaire (FFQ), food diary, weighed food record
   - Anthropometry: WHO standards, z-scores, body composition analysis
   - Biomarker analysis: serum micronutrients, inflammatory markers, metabolic panels
   - RCT nutritional intervention with blinding and placebo control
   - Metabolomics profiling (LC-MS/MS, NMR-based)
   - Nutrigenomics and gene-diet interaction studies
   - Body composition: Bioelectrical Impedance Analysis (BIA), Dual-energy X-ray Absorptiometry (DXA)
   - Gut microbiome analysis (16S rRNA sequencing, shotgun metagenomics)
   - Digital dietary assessment (photo-based, AI-assisted)
   - Doubly labeled water for energy expenditure validation

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics with statistical measures (mean, SD, 95% CI, p-values)
   - Comparison with baselines/controls
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Typical metrics: BMI, z-scores, nutrient adequacy ratio, dietary diversity score, hemoglobin, serum ferritin, vitamin D levels, lipid profile

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - 5-10 relevant formulas with variable definitions
   - BMI = weight (kg) / height (m)^2
   - BMR Harris-Benedict: Male = 66.5 + 13.75W + 5.003H - 6.775A; Female = 655.1 + 9.563W + 1.850H - 4.676A
   - Total Energy Expenditure TEE = BMR x PAL (Physical Activity Level)
   - %RDA = (actual intake / RDA) x 100
   - Protein Efficiency Ratio PER = weight gain (g) / protein intake (g)
   - Net Protein Utilization NPU = (retained nitrogen / intake nitrogen) x 100
   - Nutrient Adequacy Ratio NAR = actual intake / recommended intake
   - Mean Adequacy Ratio MAR = sum of NAR / number of nutrients
   - Z-score = (observed value - median reference) / SD reference
   - Dietary Diversity Score DDS = sum of food groups consumed in 24h

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian context where relevant (Indonesian dietary patterns, local foods, stunting prevalence)
   - Include WHO/FAO guidelines, Cochrane reviews, and AKG (Angka Kecukupan Gizi) Indonesia

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: study design, population/sample, nutritional intervention or assessment,
  key findings (anthropometric changes, nutrient intake, biomarker levels), statistical significance,
  and practical implications for nutrition practice.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Nutritional problem burden: global and Indonesian malnutrition data (stunting, wasting, obesity).
  - Physiological basis of nutritional requirement or deficiency.
  - Current nutritional intervention/assessment limitations.
  - Research gap: specific population, nutrient, food product, or intervention approach.
  - Objectives: clearly stated with measurable nutritional outcomes.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Tinjauan Pustaka"
  - Nutritional epidemiology: prevalence of malnutrition, micronutrient deficiencies.
  - Dietary patterns: Indonesian food consumption, dietary diversity, food habits.
  - Nutrient metabolism: absorption, bioavailability, interactions.
  - Nutritional interventions: supplementation, fortification, dietary counseling.
  - Food science: nutrient composition, processing effects, functional properties.
  - Policy context: Indonesian nutrition programs (PMT, MPASI, fortifikasi tepung terigu).

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Research Methodology" / "Metode Penelitian"
  - Study design: RCT, quasi-experimental, cross-sectional, cohort, case-control.
  - Population: age group, nutritional status criteria, inclusion/exclusion.
  - Dietary assessment: 24h recall (2-3 days), FFQ, food diary, food weighing.
  - Anthropometry: weight, height, MUAC, skinfold thickness (standardized protocol).
  - Biochemical: blood sampling, serum analysis (Hb, ferritin, retinol, 25(OH)D, lipid profile).
  - Body composition: BIA, DXA, skinfold calipers.
  - Nutrient analysis: Nutrisurvey, NutriClin, USDA database, Indonesian food composition table (TKPI).
  - Statistical analysis: paired t-test, ANOVA, ANCOVA, multiple regression, chi-square.
  - Ethical approval: institutional ethics committee, informed consent.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Nutrient intake: energy (kcal), protein (g), fat (g), carbohydrate (g), micronutrients.
  - Anthropometric changes: weight gain, height increment, BMI change, z-score improvement.
  - Biomarker levels: Hb (g/dL), ferritin (ng/mL), vitamin D (ng/mL), albumin (g/dL).
  - Dietary adequacy: %RDA for key nutrients, dietary diversity score.
  - Comparison with AKG (Angka Kecukupan Gizi) Indonesia.
  Tables: nutrient intake comparison, anthropometric data, biomarker levels pre-post.
  Figures: nutrient adequacy bar charts, growth curves, dietary pattern pie charts.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Nutrient adequacy radar chart (%RDA for multiple nutrients).
  - Growth curve with WHO reference lines.
  - Dietary pattern pie chart (macronutrient distribution).
  - Pre-post intervention comparison bar chart.
  - Correlation scatter plot (nutrient intake vs biomarker).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Nutrient intake: energy | protein | fat | carbohydrate | fiber | key micronutrients (mean +/- SD).
  - Anthropometric data: weight | height | BMI | z-scores (pre-post comparison).
  - Biomarker levels: Hb | ferritin | vitamin D | albumin | lipid profile (pre-post).
  - Dietary diversity: food group | frequency | % consuming | mean servings.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Direct answer to nutritional research objectives.
  - Practical implications for dietary recommendations or nutrition programs.
  - Comparison with national nutrition guidelines (AKG, Pedoman Gizi Seimbang).
  - Limitations: dietary assessment bias, short duration, single-site.
  - Future: larger RCT, longer follow-up, multi-site, biomarker validation.
