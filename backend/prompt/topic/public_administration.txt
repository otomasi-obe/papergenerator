ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: public administration phenomenon studied (service delivery, policy implementation, governance reform), theoretical framework, methodology, unit of analysis, key findings, and practical implications for public sector improvement.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Context: Indonesian bureaucratic reform (reformasi birokrasi), decentralization, New Public Management vs. New Public Service
  - Theoretical framework: NPM (Hood), NPS (Denhardt & Denhardt), governance (Rhodes), institutional theory (North)
  - Research gap: understudied local context, new digital transformation, post-pandemic governance
  - Research question with clear dependent and independent variables

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Tinjauan Pustaka" / "Literature Review"
  - Public service theories: NPM, NPS, Public Value (Moore), co-production (Ostrom)
  - Governance: good governance (UNDP), collaborative governance (Ansell & Gash), network governance
  - Policy implementation: Van Meter & Van Horn, Edwards III, Grindle
  - E-government: maturity models, digital transformation, SPBE framework
  - Indonesian context: ASN Law, village governance, regional autonomy, Ombudsman RI

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Metode Penelitian" / "Research Methodology"
  - Qualitative: case study (single/multiple), phenomenology, grounded theory, ethnography
  - Quantitative: survey (Likert scale), SEM-PLS, multiple regression, DEA
  - Mixed methods: sequential explanatory, convergent parallel
  - Data sources: government reports (LAKIP, LKJIP), surveys, interviews, FGD
  - Sampling: purposive (qualitative), proportional stratified random (quantitative)
  - Analysis: thematic analysis, content analysis, SmartPLS, SPSS, NVivo

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Service quality: SERVQUAL gap scores (-2.0 to +1.0), IKM scores (1.00-4.00)
  - Efficiency: DEA scores (0-1), input-output ratios
  - SEM: path coefficients (Beta), R-squared, Q-squared, f-squared, model fit (SRMR < 0.08)
  - Policy implementation: success rate %, compliance indicators
  - Governance index: IKLH, IPM, governance effectiveness scores
  Tables: gap analysis matrix, DEA efficiency scores, SEM results
  Figures: path diagrams, radar charts, trend analysis
