ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. Reports: animal species (cattle, broiler, layer, goat), treatment (feed additive,
  management system, genetic line), experimental design, key production parameters (ADG g/day,
  FCR, egg production %, milk yield L/day), statistical significance.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Protein demand growth and livestock's role in meeting it.
  - Current production constraints (feed cost, growth rate, reproductive efficiency).
  - Specific problem: antibiotic replacement, efficiency improvement, genetic potential.
  - Research objectives with specific measurable parameters.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review"
  - Ruminant/monogastric nutrition: energy (TDN, ME), protein (CP, RDP, RUP), fiber (NDF, ADF).
  - Feed additives: probiotics, prebiotics, enzymes, essential oils — mechanism and previous studies.
  - Genetics: estimated breeding values (EBV), heritability (h²), genetic correlation.
  - Reproduction: estrus cycle, conception rate, calving interval.
  - References: NRC (Nutrient Requirements), AOAC methods, previous trials.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods"
  - Animals: species, breed, age, initial body weight (mean ± SD).
  - Housing: individual/group pens, controlled temperature if applicable.
  - Experimental design: CRD or RCBD; 4 treatments × 5 replications.
  - Feed: basal diet composition (DM, CP, TDN), proximate analysis (AOAC).
  - Treatments: additive levels (0, 0.5, 1.0, 1.5 g/kg feed).
  - Variables: average daily gain (ADG g/day), feed intake (g/day), FCR, carcass percentage (%), egg production (%), HDP, egg quality (Haugh unit, shell thickness mm), milk yield.
  - Statistical: ANOVA, DMRT 5%, orthogonal polynomial for dose-response.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Broiler ADG: control 45–55 g/day; best treatment 60–70 g/day; FCR 1.5–1.8.
  - Layer egg production: 75–85% HDP; Haugh unit 80–90; shell thickness 0.35–0.40 mm.
  - Ruminant: cattle ADG 0.6–1.2 kg/day; milk yield 10–25 L/day.
  - FCR improvement: 8–15% with best feed additive treatment.
  Tables: feed composition, production performance means with DMRT.
  Figures: growth curve, FCR comparison bar chart, egg quality spider chart.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Growth curve: body weight over time (days) by treatment.
  - Bar chart: ADG, FCR, carcass % by treatment.
  - Spider chart: egg quality parameters.
  - Feed intake vs. body weight gain scatter plot.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Feed proximate analysis (DM, CP, CF, EE, ash, TDN).
  - Performance parameters: ADG | feed intake | FCR | carcass % by treatment.
  - Egg quality: Haugh unit | yolk color | shell thickness | shell weight.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Identifies optimal treatment level (e.g., "probiotic at 1.0 g/kg significantly improved ADG").
  - Economic implication: feed cost per kg gain.
  - Limitations: single season, single breed, pen effect.
  - Future: dose optimization, combination treatments, long-term safety.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  ADG, FCR, TDN, ME, CP, NDF, ADF, DCAD, HDP, Haugh unit, DM,
  probiotic, prebiotic, EBV, heritability, carcass percentage, dressing percentage,
  rumen, monogastric, broiler, layer, ruminant, AOAC

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  CRD, ANOVA, DMRT, proximate analysis (AOAC), performance trial, feeding trial,
  carcass analysis, egg quality evaluation, semen evaluation, fertility trial
