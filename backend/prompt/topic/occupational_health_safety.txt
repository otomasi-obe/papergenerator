   - Comparison with baselines/controls
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Typical metrics: accident frequency rate, severity rate, REBA/RULA scores, noise levels (dBA), chemical concentrations (ppm/mg/m³), safety climate scores

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - 5-10 relevant formulas with variable definitions
   - RPN = S × O × D (Risk Priority Number in FMEA)
   - Noise Dose D = (C1/T1 + C2/T2 + ... + Cn/Tn) × 100%
   - WBGT = 0.7Tnwb + 0.2Tg + 0.1Tdb (Heat Stress Index)
   - Frequency Rate FR = (Number of accidents × 10^6) / Total man-hours worked
   - Severity Rate SR = (Lost workdays × 10^6) / Total man-hours worked
   - NIOSH RWL = LC × HM × VM × DM × AM × FM × CM
   - Lifting Index LI = Load Weight / RWL
   - Safe Man Score SMS = (Safe behaviors / Total observed behaviors) × 100%
   - Incident Rate IR = (Number of incidents × 200,000) / Total hours worked
   - Risk Level R = Likelihood × Consequence

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: workplace setting (manufacturing, construction, mining, healthcare),
  hazard type (physical, chemical, biological, ergonomic, psychosocial), assessment method,
  key findings (risk levels, prevalence, compliance rates), and recommendations.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Global/national occupational accident statistics (ILO, BPS, BPJS Ketenagakerjaan data).
  - Workplace hazard identification and regulatory framework (UU No. 1/1970, PP 50/2012 SMK3).
  - Research gap: specific industry, hazard type, or intervention not yet studied in Indonesian context.
  - Objectives: "To assess/evaluate/analyze the effect of X on Y in Z workplace."

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Tinjauan Pustaka"
  - Occupational health and safety management systems (SMK3, ISO 45001, OHSAS 18001).
  - Hazard identification methods: HIRADC, JSA, HAZOP, What-If Analysis.
  - Risk assessment: qualitative, semi-quantitative, quantitative approaches.
  - Ergonomic risk factors: repetitive motion, awkward posture, manual handling.
  - Industrial hygiene: TLV, PEL, biological exposure indices.
  - Safety culture and behavior-based safety (BBS).

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Research Methodology" / "Metode Penelitian"
  - Study design: cross-sectional, case-control, cohort, quasi-experimental, action research.
  - Population and sampling: workers in specific department/industry.
  - Instruments: questionnaires (Nordic Body Map, NOSACQ-50), measurement tools (sound level meter, lux meter, heat stress monitor).
  - Ergonomic assessment: REBA, RULA, OWAS, NIOSH Lifting Equation, Strain Index.
  - Risk assessment: HIRADC matrix, FMEA, bow-tie analysis.
  - Statistical analysis: chi-square, logistic regression, Spearman correlation, paired t-test.
  - Ethical considerations: informed consent, institutional approval.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Risk levels: low, medium, high, extreme (with percentage distribution).
  - REBA scores: 1-3 (low), 4-7 (medium), 8-10 (high), 11-15 (very high).
  - Noise levels: office 40-60 dBA, manufacturing 80-95 dBA, construction 85-110 dBA.
  - Accident frequency rate: manufacturing 5-15 per million man-hours.
  - Safety climate scores: 1-5 Likert scale, mean typically 3.0-4.2.
  Tables: risk assessment matrix, ergonomic score summary, hazard inventory.
  Figures: risk map, noise contour map, before-after intervention comparison.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Risk matrix heatmap (likelihood × consequence).
  - Noise contour map of workplace.
  - Body map showing musculoskeletal complaint distribution.
  - Bow-tie diagram for critical hazard scenarios.
  - Safety performance trend chart (monthly accident rates).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - HIRADC register: hazard | risk | likelihood | consequence | risk level | control.
  - Ergonomic assessment: worker | task | REBA score | risk level | action needed.
  - Noise measurement: location | Leq dBA | duration | dose % | compliance.
  - FMEA: failure mode | severity | occurrence | detection | RPN | action.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Summarizes key risk levels and most critical hazards identified.
  - Recommends hierarchy of controls (elimination, substitution, engineering, administrative, PPE).
  - Compliance status with Indonesian regulations and international standards.
  - Limitations: cross-sectional design, single workplace, self-reported data.
  - Future: longitudinal intervention studies, multi-site comparison, technology integration.
