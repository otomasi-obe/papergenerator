EQUATIONS AND FORMULAS:
- Michaelis-Menten kinetics: v = Vmax[S] / (Km + [S])
  where v = reaction velocity, Vmax = maximum velocity, [S] = substrate concentration, Km = Michaelis constant
- Specific growth rate: μ = ln(X2/X1) / (t2 - t1)
  where X = biomass concentration, t = time
- Volumetric productivity: Qp = ΔP / (V × Δt)
  where ΔP = product formed, V = volume, Δt = time interval
- Relative gene expression: 2^(-ΔΔCt) = 2^(-(ΔCt_treatment - ΔCt_control))
  where ΔCt = Ct_target - Ct_reference
- Monod equation: μ = μmax × S / (Ks + S)
  where μmax = maximum specific growth rate, S = limiting substrate, Ks = half-saturation constant
- Yield coefficient: Y_X/S = ΔX / ΔS
  where ΔX = biomass produced, ΔS = substrate consumed
- PCR efficiency: E = 10^(-1/slope) - 1
- Transformation efficiency: TE = (colonies / μg DNA) × (total volume / plated volume)
- Enzyme activity: U = (ΔA × Vt) / (ε × d × Vs × t)
  where ΔA = absorbance change, Vt = total volume, ε = molar extinction coefficient, d = path length, Vs = sample volume, t = time
- Lineweaver-Burk: 1/v = (Km/Vmax)(1/[S]) + 1/Vmax
