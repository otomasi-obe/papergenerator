EQUATIONS:
- S = 1/((1-P) + P/N) (Amdahl's law for speedup)
- P_dynamic = alpha*C*V_dd^2*f (dynamic power consumption)
- P_static = I_leak*V_dd (static/leakage power)
- BW = width*freq*efficiency (effective bandwidth)
- CPI = sum(CPI_i * freq_i) (weighted average CPI)
- Latency = N_stages * T_clk (pipeline latency)
- Throughput = 1/max(T_stage) (pipeline throughput)
- TOPS/W = (2*MAC_ops*freq)/(P_total) (energy efficiency)
- SNR = 6.02*N + 1.76 dB (ADC SNR for N bits)
- Area_efficiency = Operations/(mm^2) (compute density)
