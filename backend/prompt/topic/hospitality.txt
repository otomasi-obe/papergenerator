ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Industry context, market pressure, operational challenge, and stakeholder relevance.
  - Clear research gap tied to a specific setting, population, technology, or managerial decision.
  - Objectives, research questions, hypotheses, and expected contribution.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Theoretical Framework"
  - Core theories, constructs, prior empirical findings, measurement scales, and contradictions.
  - Recent digital, sustainability, resilience, and customer/employee behavior perspectives.
  - Conceptual model linking antecedents, mediators, moderators, and outcomes.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Methodology" / "Methods"
  - Design: survey, interview, case study, experiment, SEM, regression, optimization, or mixed methods.
  - Sampling: purposive, stratified, convenience, panel, archival, or transaction data.
  - Instruments: validated questionnaire, secondary dataset, observation checklist, or interview guide.
  - Analysis: reliability, validity, EFA/CFA, SEM, regression, ANOVA, thematic coding, or scenario modeling.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Report descriptive statistics, reliability, model fit, hypothesis testing, and effect sizes.
  - Interpret managerial, policy, and theoretical implications by stakeholder group.
  Tables: construct measures, respondent profile, model results, robustness checks.
  Figures: conceptual model, process map, performance dashboard, category comparison.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Conceptual framework or research model.
  - Performance trend, heatmap, journey map, process flow, or dashboard.
  - Mediation/moderation diagram where relevant.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Variable definitions and measurement items.
  - Reliability/validity results and hypothesis testing summary.
  - Managerial implications by priority and feasibility.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Restate main empirical contribution and practical decision value.
  - Explain limitations in sample, time horizon, measurement, and generalizability.
  - Recommend replication, longitudinal data, cross-country testing, or advanced analytics.
