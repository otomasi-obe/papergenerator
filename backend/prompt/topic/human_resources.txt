FIELD: Business / Management
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA
SUBDISCIPLINE: human resource management, talent acquisition, training and development, performance management, compensation, employee engagement, labor relations, DEI, HR analytics, strategic HRM

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words describing the HR problem, organization or sector, employee population, theoretical model, variables, data source, method, and managerial contribution. Abstracts often report sample size, instrument reliability, analysis method such as SEM or regression, key findings on employee outcomes, and implications for HR policy.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Konteks organisasi, pasar tenaga kerja, perubahan teknologi, regulasi, dan dinamika karyawan.
  - Masalah utama seperti turnover, engagement rendah, skill gap, bias rekrutmen, konflik kerja, atau efektivitas pelatihan.
  - Gap riset pada konteks industri, model teoritis, metode pengukuran, atau bukti longitudinal.
  - Tujuan riset untuk menguji hubungan variabel, mengevaluasi program HR, atau merancang kebijakan people management.
  - Kontribusi pada teori perilaku organisasi, strategi HRM, dan praktik manajerial.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Related Work" / "Background" / sesuai bidang
  - Teori utama: social exchange theory, AMO framework, job demands-resources, psychological contract, equity theory, self-determination theory, human capital theory.
  - Studi terdahulu tentang recruitment, selection, training, performance appraisal, compensation, engagement, wellbeing, DEI, dan HR analytics.
  - Model konseptual berisi antecedent, mediator, moderator, dan outcome seperti performance, commitment, satisfaction, turnover intention, dan innovation behavior.
  - Perdebatan tentang remote work, AI in HR, employee surveillance, algorithmic fairness, pay transparency, dan green HRM.
  - Gap dan tren mutakhir pada longitudinal HR analytics, evidence-based HR, dan employee experience.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Methodology" / "Methods" / sesuai bidang
  - Desain survei kuantitatif, wawancara kualitatif, mixed-method, quasi-experiment, program evaluation, case study, atau HR analytics.
  - Data berupa kuesioner karyawan, HRIS, appraisal records, absenteeism, turnover, training scores, compensation records, dan interview transcripts.
  - Instrumen menggunakan skala tervalidasi, Cronbach alpha, composite reliability, AVE, HTMT, dan pilot testing.
  - Analisis menggunakan regression, SEM/PLS-SEM, mediation/moderation, multilevel modeling, survival analysis, thematic analysis, atau difference-in-differences.
  - Etika mencakup informed consent, anonimisasi, akses data HRIS, minimisasi data, dan perlindungan pekerja rentan.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Hasil berupa reliability/validity, descriptive statistics, hypothesis testing, path coefficients, effect sizes, model fit, dan thematic findings.
  - Diskusi menafsirkan implikasi untuk kebijakan HR, praktik manajer lini, employee experience, dan kinerja organisasi.
  - Benchmark dapat membandingkan unit bisnis, generasi, sektor, program HR, atau periode sebelum-sesudah intervensi.
  - Implikasi mencakup talent strategy, retention, wellbeing, fairness, compliance, dan digital HR transformation.
  Tables: respondent demographics, reliability and validity, correlation matrix, hypothesis results, intervention outcomes.
  Figures: conceptual model, SEM path diagram, engagement trend, HR dashboard, employee journey map.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - HR conceptual model with predictors, mediators, moderators, and outcomes.
  - SEM or PLS path diagram with standardized coefficients.
  - Employee engagement, turnover, absenteeism, or training trend chart.
  - Workforce dashboard or skills heatmap.
  - Employee lifecycle or journey map.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Sample demographic profile by age, gender, tenure, department, role, and contract type.
  - Measurement model table with loadings, Cronbach alpha, CR, AVE, and HTMT.
  - Correlation and descriptive statistics matrix.
  - Hypothesis testing table with coefficients, t-values, p-values, confidence intervals, and effect sizes.
  - HR program evaluation table with baseline, intervention, outcome, and cost.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Summarizes evidence for HR relationships, program effectiveness, or policy outcomes.
  - States theoretical and managerial contributions.
  - Notes limitations in cross-sectional design, common method bias, sample generalizability, self-report data, and access to HRIS.
  - Recommends future longitudinal, experimental, multilevel, cross-cultural, or analytics-based HR research.
