================================================================================
TOPIC GUIDE — Ecology & Conservation Biology
================================================================================
FIELD: Agricultural and Biological Sciences / Ecology
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA
SUBDISCIPLINE: Ekologi Populasi, Ekologi Komunitas, Konservasi Alam, Biodiversitas, Ekosistem

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. Reports: study ecosystem/habitat, taxa studied, methods (transect, quadrat,
  camera trap), key findings (species richness, diversity index H', population size estimate,
  threat level), and conservation implications.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Global biodiversity crisis context (extinction rates, habitat loss).
  - Specific ecosystem/taxon under threat and why it matters.
  - Research gap: unknown population status, ecological role, response to disturbance.
  - Objectives: "To assess species diversity, abundance, and conservation status of..."

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review"
  - Biodiversity theory: species-area relationship, island biogeography (MacArthur & Wilson).
  - Diversity indices: Shannon-Wiener (H'), Simpson (D), Margalef richness.
  - Habitat assessment: vegetation structure, disturbance indicators.
  - Population ecology: mark-recapture methods (Lincoln-Petersen), distance sampling.
  - Conservation biology frameworks: IUCN Red List criteria, protected area design.
  - Keystone species, trophic cascade, ecological corridor theory.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods" / "Field Methods"
  - Study site: location (GPS coordinates), habitat type, area (ha), elevation (m asl).
  - Sampling design: systematic/random transects (belt transect 20×500m, point count stations).
  - Methods by taxon: bird point count, mammal camera traps, herp pitfall traps, plant quadrats.
  - Temporal replication: 3–5 survey sessions per site.
  - Diversity analysis: Shannon-Wiener H' = -Σ(pi·ln pi); Simpson 1-D; Evenness J = H'/ln S.
  - Community analysis: cluster analysis (Bray-Curtis), NMDS ordination.
  - Population estimate: mark-recapture (Lincoln-Petersen N = M·C/R); distance sampling.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Species richness (S): forest birds 40–120 species; mammals 10–35 species.
  - Shannon diversity: H' = 2.5–3.8 (higher = more diverse).
  - Evenness: J = 0.7–0.9.
  - IUCN threatened species: 3–8 per study (VU/EN/CR categories).
  - Camera trap success rate: 5–20 events/100 trap-nights.
  Tables: species checklist with IUCN status, diversity indices by habitat.
  Figures: species accumulation curve, NMDS ordination plot, map of study sites.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Map: study sites with GPS coordinates.
  - Species accumulation curve (rarefaction curve).
  - NMDS ordination plot comparing communities.
  - Bar chart: species richness by habitat type.
  - Camera trap photo collage (for mammals).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Species list with abundance, IUCN status, endemism.
  - Diversity indices by site (S, H', D, J).
  - Habitat characteristics: canopy cover (%), basal area (m²/ha), disturbance index.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - States diversity status and conservation priority level.
  - Identifies key threats and conservation measures needed.
  - Limitations: seasonal surveys, detection probability, taxonomic uncertainty.
  - Recommendations: further surveys, habitat protection policy, community-based conservation.

ADDITIONAL SECTIONS:
  - "Conservation Implications": practical recommendations.
  - "Threat Assessment": identified threats with severity scores.
