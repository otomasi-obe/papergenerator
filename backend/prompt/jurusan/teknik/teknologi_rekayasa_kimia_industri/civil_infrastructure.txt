--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Building Information Modeling (BIM) using Revit, ArchiCAD, Tekla Structures
   - Structural analysis software (SAP2000, ETABS, STAAD.Pro, ABAQUS FEA)
   - Geotechnical investigation (CPT, SPT, geophysical survey, GIS mapping)
   - Traffic impact analysis (VISSIM, PTV Visum, HCS, SIDRA)
   - Construction management (CPM/PERT, Primavera P6, MS Project, Earned Value)
   - Green building assessment (EDGE, Greenship, LEED, BREEAM)
   - Digital twin technology for infrastructure monitoring
   - IoT-based structural health monitoring (SHM)
   - Machine learning for predictive maintenance of infrastructure
   - Drone-based inspection and photogrammetry (UAV surveying)
   - Life Cycle Assessment (LCA) for sustainable construction
   - Parametric design (Grasshopper, Dynamo) for architectural optimization

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics with statistical measures (mean, SD, 95% CI, p-values)
   - Comparison with baselines/controls
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Typical metrics: compressive strength (MPa), deflection (mm), LOS (A-F), cost overrun (%), BIM clash reduction (%), energy efficiency (kWh/m²), carbon footprint (kgCO₂/m²)

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - 5-10 relevant formulas with variable definitions
   - Beam deflection: δ = 5wL⁴/(384EI) where w=distributed load, L=span, E=modulus, I=moment of inertia
   - Column capacity: Pu = 0.85f'c(Ag-Ast) + fyAst where f'c=concrete strength, Ag=gross area, Ast=steel area, fy=yield strength
   - Structural load combination: U = 1.2D + 1.6L + 0.5(Lr or S or R) per SNI 1727
   - Concrete mix design (ACI method): w/c ratio, cement content, aggregate proportion
   - Traffic Level of Service: V/C ratio = Volume/Capacity; LOS A (<0.60) to F (>1.00)
   - Earthwork volume: V = (A₁ + A₂)/2 × d (average end area method)
   - Seismic base shear: V = Cs × W where Cs=seismic coefficient, W=building weight (SNI 1726)
   - Heat transfer: U-value = 1/(Rsi + ΣR + Rse) for building envelope thermal performance
   - Earned Value: CPI = EV/AC; SPI = EV/PV for construction project performance
   - Green building energy: EUI = Total Energy Consumption / Gross Floor Area (kWh/m²/year)

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian context (SNI building codes, earthquake zones, tropical climate, toll road development, MRT/LRT projects, flood-prone areas)
   - Include references to Indonesian infrastructure: Tol Trans-Jawa, MRT Jakarta, IKN Nusantara, dam construction programs

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: infrastructure/architectural engineering problem (structural performance,
  construction efficiency, transportation capacity, sustainability), method used (FEM analysis,
  BIM implementation, field testing, optimization), design parameters and material properties,
  key results (strength MPa, deflection mm, cost savings %, energy reduction kWh/m²),
  and engineering significance for Indonesian infrastructure development.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Indonesian infrastructure challenge: rapid urbanization, disaster vulnerability, aging structures.
  - National strategic projects: IKN Nusantara, Tol Trans-Jawa, MRT/LRT expansion, dam programs.
  - Specific engineering problem: structural inadequacy, construction delays, traffic congestion, flood vulnerability.
  - Code/standard context: SNI (Indonesian National Standard), ACI, Eurocode, AASHTO, Green Building Council Indonesia.
  - Research gap: new material/method not tested in Indonesian conditions, BIM adoption barriers, climate adaptation.
  - Objectives: design, analyze, optimize, or evaluate civil infrastructure/architectural system.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Tinjauan Pustaka"
  - Structural engineering: reinforced concrete (SNI 2847, ACI 318), steel structures (SNI 1729), seismic design (SNI 1726).
  - Construction management: project scheduling (CPM/PERT), cost estimation, risk management, lean construction.
  - Transportation: traffic analysis (HCM), pavement design (AASHTO), geometric design, public transit planning.
  - Green building: energy efficiency, thermal comfort, daylighting, EDGE/Greenship certification, sustainable materials.
  - BIM: clash detection, 4D/5D BIM, interoperability (IFC), BIM adoption in developing countries.
  - Geotechnical: soil investigation, foundation design, slope stability, ground improvement.
  - Smart infrastructure: IoT sensors, digital twins, predictive maintenance, AI-based monitoring.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Research Methodology" / "Metode Penelitian"
  - Structural analysis: FEM modeling (SAP2000/ETABS/ABAQUS), load combinations (SNI 1727, SNI 1726 seismic).
  - Material testing: concrete (SNI 1974 compressive, splitting tensile, flexural), steel tensile, aggregate gradation.
  - BIM implementation: model creation (Revit/ArchiCAD), clash detection, quantity take-off, 4D scheduling.
  - Traffic study: traffic counting, origin-destination survey, VISSIM simulation, LOS analysis.
  - Green building: energy simulation (EnergyPlus, DesignBuilder), thermal comfort (PMV/PPD), daylighting (Dialux).
  - Construction management: CPM scheduling, earned value analysis, risk assessment (Monte Carlo).
  - Geotechnical: SPT/CPT field testing, laboratory tests (triaxial, consolidation), slope stability (SLOPE/W).

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Concrete compressive strength: normal 25-35 MPa; high performance 50-80 MPa; fc' 28-day.
  - Structural deflection: within L/240 to L/360 serviceability limits.
  - BIM clash reduction: 60-85% fewer RFIs; 10-25% cost savings in coordination.
  - Traffic LOS improvement: from D/E to B/C after intervention.
  - Green building: 20-40% energy reduction; EUI from 250 to 150 kWh/m²/year.
  - Construction schedule: 15-30% time reduction with BIM/lean methods.
  - Seismic performance: inter-story drift within 2% limit; base shear capacity adequate.
  Tables: material properties, structural analysis results, traffic volume data, energy performance.
  Figures: FEM stress contours, BIM model views, traffic flow diagrams, energy consumption charts.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - FEM stress/displacement contour plots.
  - BIM 3D model with clash detection highlights.
  - Traffic flow simulation screenshots (VISSIM).
  - Structural cross-section and reinforcement details.
  - Green building energy performance comparison charts.
  - Construction schedule (Gantt chart / S-curve).

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Material properties: mix | cement | w/c | slump | fc'28 (MPa) | splitting tensile.
  - Structural results: element | load case | moment | shear | deflection | UC ratio.
  - Traffic data: intersection | volume (PCU/hr) | V/C ratio | LOS | delay (sec).
  - BIM metrics: phase | clashes detected | resolved | time saved | cost impact.
  - Energy performance: strategy | baseline (kWh/m²) | proposed | reduction (%) | payback (years).

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Engineering design meets Indonesian code requirements (SNI) with adequate safety margin.
  - Quantified improvement in performance, cost, time, or sustainability.
  - Practical recommendations for Indonesian construction industry.
  - Limitations: scale, assumptions, site-specific conditions, software limitations.
  - Future: full-scale implementation, long-term monitoring, multi-hazard assessment, digital twin deployment.

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
