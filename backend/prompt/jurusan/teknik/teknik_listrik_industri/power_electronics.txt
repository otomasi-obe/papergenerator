FIELD: Engineering / Electrical, Electronics, Communication, Automation
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: IEEE
SUBDISCIPLINE: converters, inverters, motor drives, renewable interfaces, WBG devices, digital control

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. State the electrical/electronics/communication/automation system,
  the design or analytical method, dataset/prototype/simulation setup, quantitative
  metrics, and the main performance improvement against a baseline.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Application context and engineering motivation.
  - Specific technical problem, constraints, and operating environment.
  - Gap in prior methods, topology, protocol, controller, or deployment practice.
  - Clear objectives, contribution list, and measurable success criteria.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Related Work" / "Background".
  Compare current IEEE-style research by topology, algorithm, hardware platform,
  protocol, measurement method, dataset, benchmark, and standard compliance.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "System Design and Evaluation" / "Methods".
  Use analytical modeling, MATLAB/Simulink, Python, LTspice/PSIM/PLECS, CAD/CAE,
  SDR/FPGA/MCU implementation, laboratory prototype, network testbed, or field data.
  Define variables, sampling rates, controller gains, component ratings, channel or
  plant models, calibration procedures, and reproducible evaluation scenarios.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  Report baseline comparison, sensitivity analysis, ablation study, uncertainty,
  limitations, statistical confidence where relevant, and engineering trade-offs.
  Discuss practical deployment, scalability, safety, interoperability, and standards.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - System architecture or block diagram.
  - Circuit/network/control topology.
  - Simulation or experimental waveform.
  - Performance curve versus operating condition.
  - Prototype, testbed, dashboard, or measurement setup.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Component, device, protocol, or platform specifications.
  - Parameter settings and operating conditions.
  - Baseline comparison with quantitative metrics.
  - Standards, constraints, risks, and mitigation summary.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  Summarize measured achievement, engineering significance, deployment constraints,
  limitations, and future work such as wide-bandgap devices, edge AI, digital twin,
  secure communication, resilient automation, or standards-compliant validation.
