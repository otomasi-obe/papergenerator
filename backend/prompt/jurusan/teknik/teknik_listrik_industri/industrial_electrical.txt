--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Power system analysis: ETAP, DIgSILENT PowerFactory, PSS/E, SKM PowerTools
   - Motor control design: VFD programming, soft starter selection, motor protection coordination
   - Power electronics simulation: PSIM, PLECS, MATLAB/Simulink, LTspice
   - Energy audit: ISO 50001, ASHRAE Level I/II/III, power quality analysis
   - Power quality analysis: Fluke 435-II, Hioki PQ3198, harmonic analysis, IEEE 519
   - Renewable energy integration: grid-tied inverter design, MPPT algorithms, battery management
   - Protection relay coordination: IDMT curves, directional protection, differential protection
   - Smart grid: AMI, demand response, distributed generation, microgrid control
   - Digital twin: real-time power system monitoring, predictive analytics
   - AI/ML: load forecasting, fault detection, optimal power flow

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics: voltage (V), current (A), power (kW/kVA), power factor, THD (%), efficiency (%), energy consumption (kWh), losses (kW)
   - Comparison with baselines (before vs after improvement)
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Statistical measures: mean, std deviation, confidence intervals

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - Active power: P = V×I×cosφ (single-phase), P = √3×VL×IL×cosφ (three-phase)
   - Transformer ratio: V1/V2 = N1/N2 = I2/I1
   - Motor torque: T = (P×60)/(2πn) or T = (9550×P)/n
   - Power factor: PF = P/S = cosφ
   - Efficiency: η = Pout/Pin × 100%
   - Apparent power: S = √(P² + Q²)
   - Reactive power: Q = V×I×sinφ
   - Voltage drop: ΔV = I×(R×cosφ + X×sinφ)×L
   - Short circuit current: Isc = V/(√3×Z)
   - Capacitor bank: Qc = P×(tanφ1 - tanφ2)

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian context: PLN grid, Indonesian industrial zones, rural electrification, solar/micro-hydro
   - Include Indonesian researchers and journals where applicable

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords (5-7), Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
