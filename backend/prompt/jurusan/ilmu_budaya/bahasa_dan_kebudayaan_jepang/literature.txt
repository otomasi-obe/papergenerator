FIELD: Humanities / Literature
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: MLA
SUBDISCIPLINE: literary criticism, poetry, prose fiction, drama, comparative literature, world literature, postcolonial studies, feminist criticism, narratology, digital humanities

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words naming the text/corpus, author or tradition, period, language, theoretical lens, interpretive problem, and contribution to literary criticism. Abstracts usually summarize argument, method such as close reading or corpus analysis, key motifs, and relevance to genre, form, ideology, reception, or cultural history.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Konteks karya, pengarang, genre, periode, bahasa, tradisi sastra, dan medan budaya.
  - Masalah utama berupa ambiguitas teks, motif, bentuk naratif, ideologi, resepsi, atau posisi kanon.
  - Gap riset dalam kritik terdahulu, pembacaan lokal, corpus nonkanonik, atau pendekatan teoritis.
  - Tujuan riset untuk menafsirkan, membandingkan, memetakan, atau menguji pola tekstual.
  - Kontribusi pada teori sastra, sejarah sastra, pedagogi, penerjemahan, atau digital humanities.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Related Work" / "Background" / sesuai bidang
  - Teori utama: narratology, formalism, structuralism, poststructuralism, Marxist criticism, feminism, queer theory, postcolonialism, ecocriticism, reception theory.
  - Kajian terdahulu tentang teks, pengarang, genre, motif, terjemahan, adaptasi, dan pembaca.
  - Perdebatan tentang canon, authorship, intertextuality, ideology, representation, materiality, dan world literature.
  - Metode terdahulu seperti close reading, comparative reading, corpus stylistics, distant reading, dan archival literary study.
  - Gap dan tren berupa digital humanities, environmental humanities, medical humanities, dan studi penerbitan.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Methodology" / "Methods" / sesuai bidang
  - Desain close reading, comparative literature, stylistic analysis, corpus analysis, reception study, archival research, atau mixed literary-digital method.
  - Data berupa novel, puisi, drama, cerpen, manuskrip, paratext, review, terjemahan, adaptasi, dan corpus digital.
  - Instrumen meliputi coding motif, annotation schema, concordance tools, topic modeling, network analysis, dan translation comparison.
  - Analisis menautkan bukti tekstual dengan teori, konteks historis, dan interpretasi kritis.
  - Validasi melalui transparansi corpus, kriteria pemilihan teks, triangulasi pembacaan, dan audit interpretif.

TYPICAL RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Hasil berupa argumen interpretif, pola motif, struktur naratif, representasi identitas, relasi intertekstual, atau peta resepsi.
  - Diskusi menunjukkan bagaimana bukti tekstual mendukung klaim dan memperluas teori atau kritik terdahulu.
  - Perbandingan dapat mencakup genre, periode, bahasa, terjemahan, adaptasi, dan komunitas pembaca.
  - Implikasi untuk kanon, pedagogi sastra, studi budaya, penerjemahan, atau arsip digital.
  Tables: textual comparison, motif coding, corpus metadata, theory-claim-evidence matrix.
  Figures: thematic framework, character network, frequency chart, reception timeline.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Thematic framework or conceptual reading diagram.
  - Character, motif, or intertextual network visualization.
  - Corpus frequency, collocation, or topic modeling chart.
  - Reception timeline across reviews, editions, translations, or adaptations.
  - Comparative map of settings, movements, or publication circulation.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Textual comparison table with excerpt, motif, device, context, and interpretation.
  - Corpus metadata table with author, year, genre, language, edition, and source.
  - Theory-to-evidence matrix linking concepts, passages, and critical claims.
  - Translation/adaptation comparison table with shifts in diction, form, ideology, and reception.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Restates the central literary argument and its textual evidence.
  - Identifies contribution to interpretation, theory, corpus recovery, pedagogy, or literary history.
  - Notes limitations such as corpus size, translation dependency, edition variance, and interpretive subjectivity.
  - Suggests future comparative, archival, reception, translation, or digital humanities studies.
