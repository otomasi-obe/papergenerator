FIELD: Humanities / Indonesian Literature
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: APA / MLA
SUBDISCIPLINE: Modern Indonesian Prose, Indonesian Poetry & Poetics, Drama & Performance, Postcolonial Studies, Feminist Literary Criticism, Regional Literature & Local Wisdom, Digital Literature, Comparative Literature

COMMON REFERENCE VENUES:
  Jurnal Poetika, Atavisme, Jentera, Metasastra, Litera, Diksi, Widyaparwa,
  Jurnal Ilmiah Kebudayaan Sintesis, International Journal of Literary Humanities,
  Journal of Southeast Asian Studies, Indonesia and the Malay World, Bijdragen tot
  de Taal- Land- en Volkenkunde, Wacana: Journal of the Humanities of Indonesia

--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Hermeneutics (Gadamer, Ricoeur)
   - Semiotics (Peirce triadic model, Barthes mythologies)
   - Discourse analysis (Fairclough Critical Discourse Analysis)
   - Narratology (Genette focalization, temporality, voice)
   - Postcolonial theory (Bhabha hybridity, Spivak subaltern)
   - Feminist literary criticism (Showalter gynocriticism, Cixous ecriture feminine)
   - New historicism (Greenblatt cultural poetics)
   - Digital humanities (distant reading, computational text analysis)
   - Corpus stylistics (keyword analysis, concordance, collocations)
   - Reception theory (Jauss horizon of expectations, Iser implied reader)

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics (thematic frequency, semiotic sign distribution, narrative structure mapping)
   - All data realistic and internally consistent

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS (where applicable):
   - Corpus linguistics: frequency, TF-IDF, collocational strength (MI score, t-score, log-likelihood)
   - Readability: Flesch-Kincaid, Gunning Fog
   - Inter-rater reliability: Cohen's kappa, Krippendorff's alpha
   - Sentiment analysis: polarity scores, valence distribution
   - Network analysis: centrality measures for character networks

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian/Asian context prioritized
   - Include seminal Indonesian literary scholars (Teeuw, Damono, Faruk, Mahayana)

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. State the literary problem, textual corpus, theoretical framework,
  analytical method, principal findings, contribution to Indonesian literary studies,
  limitation, and implication for future scholarship.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Define the literary phenomenon or textual problem and its cultural significance.
  - Situate within Indonesian literary history and contemporary discourse.
  - Position against current scholarship and critical debates.
  - State research gap, research questions, objectives, and expected contribution.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Kajian Pustaka" / "Tinjauan Teoretis" / "Theoretical Framework"
  - Core literary theories and critical frameworks.
  - Recent scholarship on Indonesian literature (2020-2025).
  - Textual precedents and intertextual connections.
  - Gaps in existing critical readings and opportunities for new interpretation.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Metode Penelitian" / "Research Design"
  - Design: qualitative, hermeneutic, semiotic, discourse-analytic, or mixed methods.
  - Data: textual corpus, sampling criteria, edition selection, and ethical considerations.
  - Analysis: coding scheme, theoretical lens application, triangulation strategy.
  - Metrics: inter-rater reliability, thematic saturation, analytical rigor indicators.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Present findings by research question or thematic cluster.
  - Compare interpretations with prior critical readings.
  - Discuss ambiguity, alternative readings, and contextual factors.
  - Tables: thematic distribution, semiotic sign inventory, narrative structure mapping.
  - Figures: conceptual framework, thematic network, character relationship diagram.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Theoretical framework diagram.
  - Thematic network visualization.
  - Narrative structure timeline.
  - Semiotic sign classification chart.
  - Character relationship network.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Textual corpus summary: title | author | year | genre | edition | pages.
  - Thematic coding: theme | frequency | textual evidence | interpretation.
  - Semiotic analysis: sign | object | interpretant | meaning layer.
  - Narrative structure: element | chapter/section | function | significance.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Answer research questions with specific textual evidence.
  - State theoretical and interpretive contributions.
  - Acknowledge limitations in scope, corpus selection, and generalizability.
  - Recommend future research directions with specific texts or approaches.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  Hermeneutika, Semiotika, Naratologi, Poskolonial, Feminisme Sastra, Intertekstualitas,
  Stilistika, Resepsi Sastra, Sosiologi Sastra, Ekokritik, Sastra Bandingan,
  Sastra Siber, Sastra Lisan, Lokalitas, Hibriditas Budaya

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  hermeneutic interpretation, semiotic analysis, critical discourse analysis, narratological mapping, postcolonial reading, feminist close reading, new historicist contextualization, digital text mining, corpus stylistic analysis, reception study, intertextual analysis, thematic coding, structural analysis
