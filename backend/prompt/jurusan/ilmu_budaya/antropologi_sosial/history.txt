--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature related to the user's topic
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025) that establish what is known
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame the gap as a researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Ensure the gap targets Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine the user's idea into a publishable research title
   - The title must be specific, include method and application domain
   - Target journals listed in COMMON REFERENCE VENUES below

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Use cutting-edge methods appropriate to the subdiscipline
   - Include complete research procedures and protocols
   - Specify all tools, software, instruments, and configurations
   - Detail sample size calculations, validation strategy, and reproducibility measures

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Provide realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Include statistical measures: mean, std, 95% CI, effect sizes
   - Performance/outcome metrics appropriate to the field
   - Comparison with control groups or baseline conditions
   - All numbers must be realistic, internally consistent, and domain-appropriate
   - Data must support the research conclusions logically

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - Include 5-10 relevant formulas with full variable definitions
   - Statistical tests, measurement models, analytical equations
   - Connect formulas to the methodology and results

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - Minimum 25-30 references organized thematically
   - Structure: (a) foundational theories, (b) recent advances 2020-2025, (c) limitations
   - Each subsection MUST end with: "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Use synthesis paragraphs, not citation lists
   - Include Indonesian/Southeast Asian context where relevant

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   - Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords (5-7)
   - Introduction (1500-2000 words)
   - Literature Review (2000-3000 words)
   - Methodology (2000-3000 words with formulas)
   - Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words with tables and figure descriptions)
   - Conclusion (500-800 words)
   - References (30+ entries, 60%+ from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words presenting the historical problem, periodization, geographic scope, primary sources, historiographical debate, method of source criticism, and expected contribution to understanding continuity, change, causality, agency, memory, or historical interpretation. Abstracts often clarify archival corpus, language coverage, and ethical treatment of sensitive memory.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Konteks periode, tempat, aktor, peristiwa, struktur sosial, dan signifikansi historis.
  - Masalah utama berupa interpretasi yang diperdebatkan, arsip yang belum dibaca, atau narasi dominan yang perlu diuji.
  - Gap riset dalam historiografi, ketersediaan sumber, perspektif lokal, atau pendekatan komparatif.
  - Tujuan riset untuk merekonstruksi, menjelaskan, membandingkan, atau menafsirkan proses historis.
  - Kontribusi terhadap historiografi, pemahaman publik, arsip komunitas, atau teori sejarah.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Related Work" / "Background" / sesuai bidang
  - Debat historiografis utama, konsep periodisasi, agency, struktur, memori, dan materialitas arsip.
  - Studi terdahulu berdasarkan periode, wilayah, tema, kelompok sosial, dan tradisi interpretasi.
  - Evaluasi sumber primer, bias arsip, silence, provenance, authenticity, dan representativeness.
  - Teori pendukung seperti microhistory, Annales, subaltern studies, gender history, postcolonial history, dan memory studies.
  - Tren mutakhir berupa digital history, GIS sejarah, oral history partisipatif, dan public history.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Methodology" / "Methods" / sesuai bidang
  - Desain archival research, primary source analysis, oral history, comparative history, digital humanities, atau historical GIS.
  - Data berupa arsip pemerintah, surat kabar, manuskrip, foto, peta, statistik historis, artefak, memoar, dan wawancara.
  - Kritik eksternal dan internal, triangulasi sumber, contextual reading, chronology building, dan discourse analysis.
  - Etika wawancara, izin arsip, sensitivitas komunitas, anonimisasi, dan representasi kelompok rentan.
  - Reproducibility melalui daftar arsip, kode koleksi, kriteria inklusi sumber, dan catatan transliterasi/terjemahan.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Hasil berupa rekonstruksi kronologi, interpretasi sebab-akibat, pola sosial, perubahan institusional, atau revisi historiografi.
  - Diskusi menautkan bukti primer dengan debat ilmiah dan menjelaskan batas interpretasi.
  - Perbandingan lintas wilayah/periode digunakan untuk menunjukkan kekhasan atau koneksi transnasional.
  - Implikasi untuk memori kolektif, pendidikan sejarah, kebijakan warisan, atau arsip publik.
  Tables: chronological event table, archival source matrix, actor-institution comparison.
  Figures: timeline, historical map, document facsimile, network diagram, statistical trend.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Historical timeline with key events and periodization.
  - Map of events, mobility, borders, trade routes, or conflict zones.
  - Facsimile or transcription excerpt of primary documents.
  - Actor network diagram or institutional genealogy.
  - Quantitative historical trend chart when data are available.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Chronological event table with date, location, actors, source, and interpretation.
  - Archival source list with repository, collection, code, date, language, and reliability notes.
  - Comparative historiography table across authors, arguments, evidence, and limitations.
  - Oral history participant matrix with anonymized demographics and interview context.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Restates the historical argument and evidentiary basis.
  - Clarifies contribution to historiography, method, source recovery, or public memory.
  - Notes source gaps, archive bias, translation issues, and periodization limits.
  - Recommends future archival work, comparative studies, oral history expansion, or digital public history outputs.

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
