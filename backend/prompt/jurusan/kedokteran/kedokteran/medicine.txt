================================================================================
TOPIC GUIDE — Kedokteran (Medicine / Medical Science)
================================================================================
FIELD: Medical and Health Sciences / Clinical Medicine
DEFAULT CITATION STYLE: Vancouver
SUBDISCIPLINE: Ilmu Penyakit Dalam, Bedah, Penyakit Infeksi & Tropis, Onkologi, Neurologi & Psikiatri, Kardiologi, Pediatri, Kedokteran Darurat & Intensif

COMMON REFERENCE VENUES:
  The Lancet, NEJM, BMJ, JAMA, Annals of Internal Medicine,
  Medical Journal of Indonesia, Acta Medica Indonesiana,
  Indonesian Journal of Internal Medicine, PLOS Medicine, BMC Medicine

--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Randomized controlled trials (RCT) with adaptive designs
   - Prospective and retrospective cohort studies
   - Systematic review and meta-analysis (PRISMA 2020)
   - Genomic sequencing and whole-exome sequencing (WES)
   - Proteomics and metabolomics profiling
   - AI-assisted diagnosis (deep learning, convolutional neural networks)
   - Telemedicine and digital health interventions
   - Precision medicine and pharmacogenomics
   - Immunotherapy (CAR-T, checkpoint inhibitors)
   - Single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq)
   - Mendelian randomization for causal inference
   - Network meta-analysis and Bayesian approaches

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics with statistical measures (mean, SD, 95% CI, p-values)
   - Comparison with baselines/controls
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Typical metrics: odds ratio, relative risk, hazard ratio, NNT, sensitivity, specificity, AUC-ROC, survival rates

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - 5-10 relevant formulas with variable definitions
   - Odds Ratio OR = (a x d) / (b x c)
   - Relative Risk RR = [a/(a+b)] / [c/(c+d)]
   - Number Needed to Treat NNT = 1 / ARR (Absolute Risk Reduction)
   - Sensitivity = TP / (TP + FN)
   - Specificity = TN / (TN + FP)
   - Positive Predictive Value PPV = TP / (TP + FP)
   - Negative Predictive Value NPV = TN / (TN + FN)
   - Kaplan-Meier Survival S(t) = Pi[(ni - di) / ni]
   - Hazard Ratio HR = h1(t) / h0(t)
   - AUC-ROC for diagnostic accuracy
   - Sample size n = (Za/2 + Zb)^2 x 2s^2 / d^2

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian context where relevant (Indonesian disease burden, healthcare system, BPJS)
   - Include WHO guidelines, Cochrane reviews, and landmark trials

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

DOMAIN-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words, structured (Background, Methods, Results, Conclusion). Reports: study design,
  population/sample, intervention/exposure, primary outcome, key statistical results
  (OR/RR/HR with 95% CI, p-value), and clinical significance.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Disease burden: global and Indonesian prevalence/incidence/mortality data.
  - Pathophysiology and current understanding of disease mechanism.
  - Current treatment/diagnostic limitations and unmet clinical needs.
  - Research gap: specific population, novel biomarker, treatment comparison, or diagnostic tool.
  - Objectives: clearly stated primary and secondary endpoints.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Tinjauan Pustaka"
  - Epidemiology: global and national disease statistics.
  - Pathogenesis: molecular mechanisms, risk factors, genetic predisposition.
  - Diagnosis: current gold standard, emerging biomarkers, imaging modalities.
  - Treatment: evidence-based guidelines, novel therapies, comparative effectiveness.
  - Prognosis: survival data, prognostic factors, scoring systems.
  - Indonesian context: local disease patterns, healthcare access, BPJS coverage.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Methods" / "Metode Penelitian"
  - Study design: RCT, cohort, case-control, cross-sectional, case series.
  - Setting: hospital type (RSUP, RSUD, RS swasta), department, time period.
  - Population: inclusion/exclusion criteria, sampling method, sample size calculation.
  - Variables: independent, dependent, confounders.
  - Data collection: medical records, questionnaires, laboratory tests, imaging.
  - Statistical analysis: univariate, bivariate (chi-square, t-test, Mann-Whitney), multivariate (logistic regression, Cox regression, linear regression).
  - Ethical approval: institutional ethics committee, informed consent.
  - Reporting guidelines: CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, STARD, CARE.

EQUATIONS AND FORMULAS:
  - Odds Ratio OR = (a x d) / (b x c)
  - Relative Risk RR = [a/(a+b)] / [c/(c+d)]
  - Number Needed to Treat NNT = 1 / ARR
  - Sensitivity = TP / (TP + FN)
  - Specificity = TN / (TN + FP)
  - Positive Predictive Value PPV = TP / (TP + FP)
  - Negative Predictive Value NPV = TN / (TN + FN)
  - Kaplan-Meier Survival S(t) = Pi[(ni - di) / ni]
  - Hazard Ratio HR = h1(t) / h0(t)
  - Sample size n = (Za/2 + Zb)^2 x 2s^2 / d^2
  All variables must be defined immediately after the equation.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - CONSORT/STROBE flow diagram.
  - Kaplan-Meier survival curves.
  - Forest plot (meta-analysis).
  - ROC curve with AUC.
  - Bar/box plots for group comparisons.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Baseline characteristics (continuous: mean+/-SD; categorical: n, %).
  - Bivariate analysis: variable | case | control | OR (95% CI) | p-value.
  - Multivariate logistic regression: variable | adjusted OR | 95% CI | p-value.
  - Diagnostic accuracy: sensitivity | specificity | PPV | NPV | AUC.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Demographic characteristics: age, sex, BMI, comorbidities.
  - Primary outcome: effect size with 95% CI and p-value.
  - Subgroup analysis and sensitivity analysis.
  - Comparison with previous studies (concordant/discordant findings).
  - Biological plausibility and mechanistic explanation.
  Tables: baseline characteristics, univariate analysis, multivariate analysis, diagnostic accuracy.
  Figures: Kaplan-Meier curves, forest plots, ROC curves, flowcharts.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Direct answer to research objectives.
  - Clinical implications and recommendations.
  - Study limitations: selection bias, information bias, confounders, generalizability.
  - Future research directions.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  odds ratio, relative risk, hazard ratio, confidence interval, p-value,
  sensitivity, specificity, NNT, AUC-ROC, Kaplan-Meier, Cox regression,
  CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA, intention-to-treat, per-protocol,
  biomarker, pathogenesis, prognosis, comorbidity, differential diagnosis

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  randomized controlled trial, cohort study, case-control, cross-sectional,
  systematic review, meta-analysis, logistic regression, Cox proportional hazards,
  Kaplan-Meier survival analysis, ROC analysis, sample size calculation,
  intention-to-treat analysis, CONSORT reporting, ethical approval
