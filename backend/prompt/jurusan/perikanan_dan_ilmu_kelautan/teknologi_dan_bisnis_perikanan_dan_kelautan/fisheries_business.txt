--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title (specific, with method and domain)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Value chain analysis (VCA) with mapping and governance assessment
   - Business model canvas (BMC) and lean canvas for fisheries startups
   - SWOT-AHP (Analytic Hierarchy Process) for strategic planning
   - Financial feasibility analysis: NPV, IRR, B/C ratio, payback period
   - Market analysis: SCP (Structure-Conduct-Performance), market integration
   - Supply chain optimization with linear programming
   - Blue economy assessment framework
   - Life cycle assessment (LCA) for sustainability evaluation
   - Digital platform analysis for fisheries e-commerce
   - Spatial analysis with GIS for fisheries resource mapping
   - Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) for behavioral studies
   - Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA) for efficiency measurement

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Field-appropriate metrics with statistical measures (mean, SD, 95% CI, p-values)
   - Comparison with baselines/controls
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent
   - Typical metrics: NPV, IRR, B/C ratio, profit margin, market share, efficiency scores, value added ratio, marketing margin

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - 5-10 relevant formulas with variable definitions
   - NPV = Sum[(Bt - Ct) / (1 + r)^t] for t = 0 to n
   - IRR: rate r where NPV = 0, i.e., Sum[(Bt - Ct) / (1 + IRR)^t] = 0
   - B/C Ratio = Sum[Bt / (1 + r)^t] / Sum[Ct / (1 + r)^t]
   - Payback Period PP = n + (a - b) / (c - b), where n = last year negative cumulative, a = initial investment, b = cumulative cash flow year n, c = cumulative cash flow year n+1
   - Profit Margin = (Revenue - Total Cost) / Revenue x 100%
   - Marketing Margin MM = Pr - Pf (retail price - farm gate price)
   - Marketing Efficiency ME = (Value added / Marketing cost) x 100%
   - Herfindahl-Hirschman Index HHI = Sum(si^2), where si = market share of firm i
   - Revenue Cost Ratio R/C = Total Revenue / Total Cost
   - Break Even Point BEP = Fixed Cost / (Price per unit - Variable Cost per unit)

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian context where relevant (Indonesian fisheries production, KKP policies, WPP-NRI)
   - Include FAO reports, World Bank blue economy studies, and Indonesian fisheries statistics

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords, Introduction (1500-2000 words), Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words), Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words), References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  200-250 words. Reports: fisheries commodity/business type, location, analytical method,
  key findings (financial indicators, market structure, value chain efficiency),
  and business/policy implications.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Indonesian fisheries production statistics (KKP data, BPS).
  - Fisheries sector contribution to GDP, employment, and food security.
  - Business challenges: market access, cold chain, capital, technology adoption.
  - Research gap: specific commodity, region, business model, or technology not yet studied.
  - Objectives: "To analyze/evaluate the feasibility/value chain/market of X in Y region."

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Tinjauan Pustaka"
  - Fisheries economics: production economics, market structure, price formation.
  - Value chain: actors, governance, upgrading strategies, value distribution.
  - Business feasibility: investment analysis, financial indicators, risk assessment.
  - Blue economy: sustainable ocean economy, ecosystem services valuation.
  - Fisheries policy: IUU fishing, quota management, subsidies, trade regulations.
  - Technology adoption: cold chain, processing technology, digital platforms.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Research Methodology" / "Metode Penelitian"
  - Study design: case study, survey, mixed methods, action research.
  - Location: specific fishing port, aquaculture area, processing center, market.
  - Population: fishers, fish farmers, processors, traders, exporters.
  - Sampling: purposive, snowball, stratified random (based on business scale).
  - Data collection: structured interviews, questionnaires, financial records, market observation.
  - Analysis methods: value chain mapping, financial feasibility (NPV, IRR, B/C), SWOT-AHP, SEM, DEA.
  - Software: SPSS, SmartPLS, AMOS, R, Excel financial modeling.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Financial indicators: NPV > 0 (feasible), IRR > discount rate, B/C > 1.
  - Value chain: margin distribution (fisher 30-40%, trader 20-30%, processor 30-40%).
  - Market structure: oligopsony/oligopoly, market concentration (HHI).
  - Efficiency: technical efficiency 60-85%, allocative efficiency 50-75%.
  - Business model: revenue streams, cost structure, key partnerships.
  Tables: financial feasibility summary, value chain margin, market structure indicators.
  Figures: value chain map, business model canvas, break-even chart, sensitivity analysis.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Value chain map with actors, flows, and margins.
  - Business model canvas diagram.
  - Break-even analysis chart.
  - Sensitivity analysis tornado diagram.
  - Market channel flow diagram.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Financial feasibility: investment cost | operating cost | revenue | NPV | IRR | B/C | PP.
  - Value chain margin: actor | buying price | selling price | cost | margin | profit.
  - SWOT matrix: strengths | weaknesses | opportunities | threats.
  - Market structure: number of firms | HHI | CR4 | market type.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Financial feasibility verdict (feasible/not feasible with indicators).
  - Key value chain bottlenecks and upgrading recommendations.
  - Business strategy recommendations for stakeholders.
  - Policy implications for government (KKP, local government).
  - Limitations: single location, short time frame, limited sample.
  - Future: multi-site comparison, longitudinal study, technology intervention.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  value chain, supply chain, NPV, IRR, B/C ratio, payback period,
  marketing margin, market structure, blue economy, cold chain,
  fish landing, auction (TPI), fishing port (PPI/PPN/PPS),
  aquaculture business, seafood processing, export-import,
  IUU fishing, WPP-NRI, KKP, BKIPM, traceability

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  value chain analysis, financial feasibility study, SWOT-AHP,
  business model canvas, market structure analysis, supply chain management,
  data envelopment analysis, structural equation modeling,
  cost-benefit analysis, sensitivity analysis, break-even analysis
