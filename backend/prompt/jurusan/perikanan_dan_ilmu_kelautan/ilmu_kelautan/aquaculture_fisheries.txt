--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025)
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame as researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Target Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals (Aquaculture, Aquaculture Nutrition, Reviews in Aquaculture)

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine into publishable research title
   - Ensure novelty through species specificity, methodological innovation, or system integration
   - Cross-reference with Indonesian aquaculture context: shrimp (vannamei), milkfish (bandeng), tilapia, seaweed (Gracilaria, Kappaphycus), grouper, catfish (lele), Indonesian pond systems (tambak)

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Recirculating Aquaculture Systems (RAS) with advanced biofilter design
   - Biofloc technology (BFT) with carbon:nitrogen ratio optimization
   - Probiotics/synbiotics application (Bacillus, Lactobacillus, yeast-based)
   - CRISPR-Cas9 for aquaculture genetics and disease resistance breeding
   - Environmental DNA (eDNA) monitoring for biodiversity and pathogen detection
   - Precision aquaculture: IoT sensors, automated feeding, real-time water quality monitoring
   - Integrated Multi-Trophic Aquaculture (IMTA) system design
   - Machine learning for disease detection (computer vision, CNN for fish behavior analysis)
   - Nanobiotechnology for drug delivery and feed additives
   - Metagenomics for gut microbiome analysis in aquaculture species

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Aquaculture metrics: SGR (%/day), FCR, survival rate (%), biomass (g/m2), water quality parameters
   - Statistical measures: mean +/- SD, ANOVA F-values, p-values, Duncan/Tukey groupings
   - All numbers realistic and internally consistent with tropical aquaculture conditions

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - Specific Growth Rate: SGR = (ln W2 - ln W1)/(t2 - t1) x 100
   - Feed Conversion Ratio: FCR = total feed given (g) / total weight gain (g)
   - Survival Rate: SR = (Nt/N0) x 100%
   - Protein Efficiency Ratio: PER = weight gain (g) / protein intake (g)
   - Apparent Net Protein Utilization: ANPU = (final body protein - initial body protein) / protein intake x 100
   - Condition Factor: K = (W/L^3) x 100
   - Hepatosomatic Index: HSI = (liver weight / body weight) x 100
   - Water Quality Index: WQI = Sum(Wi x qi) / Sum(Wi)
   - Von Bertalanffy Growth: Lt = Linf(1 - e^(-K(t-t0)))
   - Thermal Growth Coefficient: TGC = (W2^(1/3) - W1^(1/3)) / (Sum(T x days)) x 1000

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - 25-30 references thematically organized
   - Each subsection ends with "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Indonesian aquaculture context: tambak systems, tropical species, local feed ingredients
   - Include recent Indonesian aquaculture studies (IPB, UNDIP, Brawijaya, BRIN publications)
   - Reference FAO aquaculture statistics and Indonesian MMAF (KKP) data

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords (5-7), Introduction (1500-2000 words),
   Literature Review (2000-3000 words), Methodology (2000-3000 words),
   Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words), Conclusion (500-800 words),
   References (30+, 60% from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150-250 words. Reports: fish/shrimp species (with Latin name), system (pond, recirculating
  aquaculture system - RAS, cage), treatments, key results (SGR %/day, FCR, survival %,
  water quality parameters), statistical design.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Global/national aquaculture production statistics (tons/year).
  - Production bottleneck: disease, feed cost, water quality, growth rate.
  - Gap: effect of specific feed ingredient/management on the target species.
  - Objectives: "To evaluate the effect of X on growth, survival, and FCR of Y."

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Review of Related Studies"
  - Nutritional requirements of the target species: protein (30-45% CP), lipid, energy.
  - Alternative protein/lipid sources: soybean meal, black soldier fly larvae (BSFL), fishmeal replacement.
  - Disease resistance and immune response: lysozyme activity, hematological parameters.
  - Water quality parameters: DO (mg/L), temperature (C), pH, ammonia (mg/L), nitrite.
  - Probiotics, prebiotics, immunostimulants in aquaculture.

METHODOLOGY:
  Common section name: "Materials and Methods"
  - Experimental fish: species, initial weight (g), stocking density (fish/m2 or /tank).
  - System: aquaria (100 L), floating cages, recirculating system.
  - Experimental design: CRD, 4 treatments x 3 replicates.
  - Feed preparation: formulated diet, proximate analysis (crude protein %, lipid %).
  - Feeding: ad libitum or 3-5% body weight/day; 2-3 times/day.
  - Duration: 56-90 days.
  - Variables: SGR = (ln Wf - ln Wi)/t x 100; FCR = feed consumed/weight gain; survival rate (%); condition factor K; protein efficiency ratio (PER).
  - Water quality: DO, pH, temperature, ammonia (measured weekly).
  - Statistical: ANOVA, DMRT 5%.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - SGR: Nile tilapia 2.5-4.5 %/day; catfish 3-5 %/day; shrimp 5-8 %/day.
  - FCR: fish 1.2-2.5; shrimp 1.5-2.0.
  - Survival rate: 80-98%.
  - Water quality: DO 5-7 mg/L; temp 26-30C; pH 6.5-8.0; ammonia <0.1 mg/L.
  Tables: growth performance table, water quality table, feed analysis table.
  Figures: growth curve, SGR comparison bar chart, survival rate chart.

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Growth curve: body weight over time.
  - Bar chart: SGR, FCR, survival rate by treatment.
  - Water quality trend chart.
  - Feed utilization efficiency diagram.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Feed proximate analysis (moisture, CP, lipid, ash, NFE, GE).
  - Growth performance: initial weight | final weight | SGR | FCR | survival %.
  - Water quality: DO | temp | pH | NH3 | NO2 (weekly mean +/- SD).

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - States optimal dietary treatment and its practical significance.
  - Economic: reduced feed cost, improved profitability.
  - Limitations: tank study vs. pond conditions; single species.
  - Future: semi-commercial scale trials, combination with probiotics.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  SGR, FCR, survival rate, condition factor, PER, stocking density, DO, RAS,
  fishmeal, soybean meal, BSFL, crude protein, lipid, proximate analysis,
  lysozyme, hematocrit, glucose, cortisol, immune response, WSSV, Aeromonas

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  CRD, ANOVA, DMRT, proximate analysis, growth trial, challenge test,
  hematological analysis, water quality monitoring, feeding trial
