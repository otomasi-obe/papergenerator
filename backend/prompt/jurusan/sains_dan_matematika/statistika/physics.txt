--- PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---
When given a user's research idea, you MUST generate a COMPLETE academic paper following these requirements:

1. RESEARCH GAP IDENTIFICATION:
   - Identify 3-5 specific gaps in current literature related to the user's topic
   - Reference recent works (2020-2025) that establish what is known
   - Clearly articulate what remains UNKNOWN or UNRESOLVED
   - Frame the gap as a researchable question with measurable outcomes
   - Ensure the gap targets Q1/Q2 Scopus-indexed journals

2. NOVEL TOPIC REFINEMENT:
   - Refine the user's idea into a publishable research title
   - The title must be specific, include method and application domain
   - Target journals listed in COMMON REFERENCE VENUES below

3. LATEST METHODOLOGY (2022-2025):
   - Use cutting-edge methods appropriate to the subdiscipline
   - Include complete algorithmic steps or research procedures
   - Specify all tools, software, versions, and configurations
   - Detail parameters, validation strategy, and reproducibility measures

4. ESTIMATED DATA & RESULTS:
   - Provide realistic estimated data tables (10-30 data points)
   - Include statistical measures: mean, std, 95% CI
   - Performance/accuracy metrics appropriate to the method
   - Comparison with 3-5 baseline/state-of-the-art methods
   - All numbers must be realistic, internally consistent, and domain-appropriate
   - Data must support the research conclusions logically

5. MATHEMATICAL FORMULAS:
   - Include 5-10 relevant formulas with full variable definitions
   - Show derivations or justifications where appropriate
   - Connect formulas to the methodology and results

6. LITERATURE REVIEW WITH EXPLICIT GAPS:
   - Minimum 25-30 references organized thematically
   - Structure: (a) foundational theories, (b) recent advances 2020-2025, (c) limitations
   - Each subsection MUST end with: "However, no study has addressed..."
   - Use synthesis paragraphs, not citation lists
   - Include Indonesian/Southeast Asian context where relevant

7. COMPLETE PAPER STRUCTURE:
   - Title, Abstract (200-250 words), Keywords (5-7)
   - Introduction (1500-2000 words)
   - Literature Review (2000-3000 words)
   - Methodology (2000-3000 words with formulas)
   - Results & Discussion (2000-3000 words with tables and figure descriptions)
   - Conclusion (500-800 words)
   - References (30+ entries, 60%+ from 2020-2025)
--- END PAPER GENERATION INSTRUCTIONS ---

DOMAIN-SPECIFIC GUIDANCE

ABSTRACT CHARACTERISTICS:
  150–250 words. State the problem, domain-specific gap, dataset/sample/object, method, quantitative result pattern, and significance.
  Prefer concrete variables, measurable outcomes, uncertainty, and practical implication.

INTRODUCTION ELEMENTS:
  - Define the scientific or mathematical problem and why it matters.
  - Summarize established theory, empirical context, and limitations of prior work.
  - Identify a precise knowledge gap, hypothesis, theorem claim, or modeling objective.
  - End with research questions, contributions, and paper structure.

LITERATURE REVIEW:
  Common section name: "Literature Review" / "Background" / "Related Work"
  - Organize by concepts, methods, applications, and unresolved debates.
  - Compare classical foundations with recent computational or empirical approaches.
  - Highlight assumptions, datasets, instruments, proofs, or validation strategies.
  - Close each subsection with synthesis rather than a list of citations.

METHODOLOGY:
  experimental measurement, theoretical derivation, simulation (COMSOL, ANSYS), Monte Carlo method, particle physics, quantum mechanics
  Report design, data/source/sample, variables, instruments/software, parameters, validation, and reproducibility steps.
  Include ethical approval, preregistration, or open-science artifacts when relevant.

EQUATIONS AND FORMULAS:
  Newton's laws, Maxwell's equations, Schrödinger equation, thermodynamic laws, Boltzmann distribution, Einstein field equation approximations

TYPICAL FIGURES:
  - Main workflow or conceptual diagram.
  - Primary result visualization with confidence or uncertainty information.
  - Diagnostic, residual, convergence, sensitivity, or validation plot.
  - Comparative figure against baseline theory, model, or prior study.

TYPICAL TABLES:
  - Dataset/sample/instrument/proof-condition summary.
  - Parameter, coefficient, theorem condition, or model specification table.
  - Performance, uncertainty, robustness, or ablation comparison table.

RESULTS & DISCUSSION:
  - Present core estimates, proofs, simulations, experimental measurements, or classification outcomes first.
  - Quantify uncertainty, error, sensitivity, robustness, and boundary conditions.
  - Compare with theoretical expectations and previous literature.
  - Discuss mechanisms, limitations, threats to validity, and external generalizability.

CONCLUSION CHARACTERISTICS:
  - Answer the research question directly.
  - State contribution to theory, method, evidence, or application.
  - Note limitations and responsible interpretation.
  - Recommend concrete follow-up studies, validation, or extensions.

DOMAIN VOCABULARY:
  Hamiltonian, Lagrangian, wavefunction, uncertainty, entropy, field, symmetry, boson, fermion, phonon, band gap, plasma, cross section, resonance, decoherence

METHODOLOGY KEYWORDS:
  experimental measurement, theoretical derivation, simulation (COMSOL, ANSYS), Monte Carlo method, particle physics, quantum mechanics
