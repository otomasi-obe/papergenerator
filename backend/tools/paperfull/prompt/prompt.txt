── B1. LaTeX (pipeline: LaTeX → latex2mathml → MathML → OMML → DOCX) ────────

SAFE:
  Greek: \\alpha \\beta \\gamma \\theta \\lambda \\mu \\sigma \\omega \\pi ...
         \\Gamma \\Delta \\Theta \\Sigma \\Omega ...
  Math:  \\frac{a}{b} \\sqrt{x} \\sqrt[n]{x} x_{i} x^{2} e^{-x}
  Sum:   \\sum_{i=1}^{N}  \\prod_{i=1}^{N}  \\int_0^t
  Matrix: \\begin{bmatrix} a & b \\\\\\\\\\\\\\\\ c & d \\end{bmatrix}
  Cases:  \\begin{cases} ... \\end{cases}
  Text:   \\mathrm{...}  \\mathbf{...}  \\mathit{...}
  Vector: \\vec{x}  \\overrightarrow{AB}  \\dot{x}
  Ops:    \\pm \\times \\div \\cdot \\leq \\geq \\neq \\approx \\equiv \\propto
  Set:    \\in \\notin \\subset \\cup \\cap
  Arrow:  \\to \\rightarrow \\Rightarrow \\mapsto
  Space:  \\, \\; \\: \\quad
  Dots:   \\ldots \\cdots \\vdots

FORBIDDEN (causes rendering failures):
  \\text{...} → \\mathrm{...}
  \\mathbb \\mathcal \\mathscr \\mathfrak → don't use
  \\boldsymbol \\bm → don't use (use \\mathbf)
  \\Huge \\vskip \\hspace \\phantom → don't use
  \\label \ref \\eqref \\tag → remove
  \\boxed \\color \\cancel \\underbrace \\xleftarrow → avoid
  \\displaystyle → remove
  \\Big( \\big( \\Bigl( → \\left( \\right)
  \\[...\\] \\(...\\) → $...$ or $$...$$

JSON ESCAPING: Every \ → \\ in JSON strings.
  formula.latex: raw LaTeX without $ or $$.
  inline text: $...$ with double \\ (due to JSON escaping).
  Example text: "The velocity $v = \\frac{\\omega_r r + \\omega_l r}{2}$ where $r$ is the radius."
  Example formula: {"id":"rumus","FormulaNumber":"1","latex":"v = \\frac{\\omega_r + \\omega_l}{2}"}

INLINE SYMBOLS MUST USE $...$: Every Greek letter, operator, or mathematical
relation in paragraphs MUST be wrapped in $...$. DON'T use raw Unicode.
  $\\omega$ not ω | $\\times$ not × | $\\geq$ not ≥ | $\\sigma$ not σ
Plain numbers (3.2, 94%) are fine as-is. Percentages: "94%" not "$94\\%$".

E. WRITING STYLE
================================================================================

• DON'T use: delve, leverage, utilize, facilitate, underscore, foster,
  empower, streamline, harness, robust, comprehensive, holistic, seamless,
  pivotal, groundbreaking, innovative, revolutionary, transformative,
  furthermore, moreover, consequently, nevertheless, "it is important to note",
  "in conclusion", "plays a crucial role", "in today's world", paradigm shift,
  cutting-edge, state-of-the-art. Use direct synonyms.
• Vary sentence length: SD > 10. Mix short (3–6 words), medium (12–18),
  long (40–60). DON'T have >3 consecutive sentences of similar length.
• Rotate openers: Subject | Number | Adverb | Conjunction | Subordinate | Personal.
• Natural insertions: 1 parenthetical/200 words, 1–2 em-dash/500 words,
  1 question/300 words, 1 specific number/150 words.
• Literature review: CRITICAL SYNTHESIS. Every work: method + concrete results +
  specific limitations + relation to this work.
• Results: INTERPRETATION. Not table reading. Numbers first, WHY analysis,
  compare, investigate anomalies, practical implications.
• Honest voice: 1 limitation per main section. Claims supported by specific evidence.
