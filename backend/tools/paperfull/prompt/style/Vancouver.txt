================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — Vancouver (Numbered, Medicine & Health Sciences)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper. Default for medicine, nursing,
dentistry, pharmacy, and biomedical sciences.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source     :  "...the treatment was effective [1]."
  Two sources       :  "...demonstrated in trials [1,2]."
  Three or more     :  "...supported by multiple studies [1,2,3]."
  Consecutive range :  "...shown across studies [2-5]."
  Mixed             :  "...reported in [2,3,6-8]."
  Author in sentence:  "Smith et al. [4] reported that..."
  Rules:
    - Numbered in square brackets: [1], [2], [3]
    - Numbered sequentially in order of first appearance in text
    - Consecutive numbers collapsed with hyphen: [3-6]
    - Non-consecutive separated with commas: [1,3,7]
    - NO spaces between numbers and brackets

── REFERENCE LIST FORMAT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Numbered [1], [2]... in order cited. NOT alphabetical.

  Journal Article:
    [N] Lastname AB, Lastname CD. Title of article in sentence case.
        Abbreviated Journal Name. Year;Volume(Issue):Page-Page.
        doi: 10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Book (entire):
    [N] Lastname AB, Lastname CD. Title of Book. Edition ed. City:
        Publisher; Year.

  Book Chapter:
    [N] Lastname AB. Chapter title. In: Lastname AB, editor. Book Title.
        City: Publisher; Year. p. XX-YY.

  Conference Paper:
    [N] Lastname AB. Title of paper. In: Conference Name; Year Month Date;
        City, Country. Publisher; Year. p. XX-YY.

  Thesis:
    [N] Lastname AB. Title of thesis [PhD dissertation / Master's thesis].
        City: University Name; Year.

  Website:
    [N] Lastname AB. Title of page [Internet]. City: Organization; Year
        [cited Year Month Day]. Available from: https://www.example.com

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Author names: Lastname followed by initials without periods (Smith AB)
  • Journal names: abbreviated using standard NLM/MEDLINE abbreviations
  • Article titles: sentence case
  • Journal titles: abbreviated, no italics required
  • Year;Volume(Issue):Pages — no spaces around semicolon and colon
  • List all authors if 6 or fewer; if 7 or more, list first 6 then "et al."
  • Minimum 20 references; ALL cited in body text
  • Use real medical/health journal names:
      Medicine    : N Engl J Med, JAMA, BMJ, Lancet, Ann Intern Med
      Nursing     : J Adv Nurs, Int J Nurs Stud, Nurs Res
      Dentistry   : J Dent Res, J Clin Periodontol, Oral Dis
      Pharmacy    : J Clin Pharm Ther, Br J Clin Pharmacol
      Public Health: Am J Public Health, Bull World Health Organ
