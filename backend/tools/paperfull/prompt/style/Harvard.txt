================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — Harvard (Author-Date)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source           :  "...the system is effective (Santoso, 2020)."
  Author in sentence      :  "Santoso (2020) found that..."
  Two authors             :  "...shown by (Smith & Jones, 2022)."
  Three or more authors   :  "...demonstrated (Garcia et al., 2021)."
  Two sources             :  "...multiple studies (Lee, 2019; Kim, 2021)."
  With page number        :  "...as stated (Smith, 2020, p. 45)."
  Rules:
    - Format: (Author, Year) — comma between author and year
    - Use "&" inside parentheses; "and" outside
    - "et al." for three or more authors
    - Separate multiple sources with semicolons, chronological or alphabetical order
    - For direct quotes, add page: (Smith, 2020, p. 45)

── REFERENCE LIST FORMAT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Title: "References". Alphabetical by last name. Hanging indent.

  Journal Article:
    Lastname, A.B. & Lastname, C.D. (Year) 'Title of article in sentence case',
        Journal Name in Italics, Volume(Issue), pp. AAA–BBB.
        doi: 10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Conference Paper:
    Lastname, A.B. (Year) 'Paper title in sentence case', in Proceedings of
        Conference Name (Location, Date). City: Publisher, pp. XX–YY.

  Book (entire):
    Lastname, A.B. (Year) Title of Book in Title Case. City: Publisher.

  Book Chapter:
    Lastname, A.B. (Year) 'Chapter title in sentence case', in A.B. Editor
        (ed.) Book Title in Title Case. City: Publisher, pp. XX–YY.

  Thesis:
    Lastname, A.B. (Year) Title of Thesis. PhD thesis / Master's dissertation,
        University Name.

  Website:
    Lastname, A.B. (Year) Title of webpage [Online]. Available at:
        https://www.example.com (Accessed: Day Month Year).

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Journal titles: in italics or bold
  • Article titles: in 'single quotation marks'
  • Volume in italics; issue number in parentheses, not italicized
  • Page numbers with "pp." prefix
  • Minimum 20 references; ALL cited in body text
  • Use real journal names across disciplines:
      Business/Management : Journal of Management, Harvard Business Review,
                            Strategic Management Journal, Academy of Management Review
      Education           : British Journal of Education, Teaching in Higher Education,
                            Studies in Higher Education, Educational Research
      Health Sciences     : The Lancet, BMJ Open, Journal of Nursing Studies,
                            Public Health Nutrition, Health Education Research
      Social Work         : British Journal of Social Work, Social Work Research,
                            Journal of Social Work

── STRUCTURED REFERENCE OUTPUT (CRITICAL) ─────────────────────────────────────
  References MUST be output as structured JSON objects with full metadata,
  NOT as pre-formatted strings. This allows the system to reformat references
  to ANY citation style automatically.

  Each reference in the "references"."items" array MUST be an object:
  {
    "authors": ["Lastname1, Initials1", "Lastname2, Initials2"],
    "year": 2023,
    "title": "Full title of the article/paper/book",
    "type": "journal|conference|book|book_chapter|thesis|website",
    "journal": "Journal Name (for journal articles)",
    "conference": "Conference Name (for conference papers)",
    "volume": "XX",
    "issue": "Y",
    "pages": "AAA-BBB",
    "doi": "10.XXXX/XXXXXXX",
    "publisher": "Publisher Name (for books/theses)",
    "location": "City, Country (for books/theses)",
    "url": "https://... (for websites)",
    "accessed": "DD Mon YYYY (for websites)",
    "editors": ["Editor Name (for book chapters)"],
    "book_title": "Book Title (for book chapters)",
    "institution": "University Name (for theses)"
  }

  Only include fields relevant to the reference type.
  DO NOT output references as formatted strings like "[1] A. Smith, 'Title'..."
  DO NOT include "_CATATAN" or "_ATURAN_REFERENSI" fields in output.
