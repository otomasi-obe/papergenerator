================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — ACS (American Chemical Society)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper. Default for chemistry,
biochemistry, chemical engineering, and materials science papers.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source     :  "...the compound was synthesized [1]."  or  "...1."
  Two sources       :  "...shown in prior work [1,2]."
  Three or more     :  "...demonstrated [1,2,3]."
  Consecutive range :  "...reported [2-5]."
  Author in sentence:  "Smith et al. [4] reported that..."
  Rules:
    - Numbered superscripts or numbers in square brackets
    - Numbered sequentially in order of first appearance
    - Consecutive ranges with hyphen: [3-6]
    - Superscript form (preferred): Smith et al.⁴ or ...compound.⁴

── REFERENCE LIST FORMAT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Numbered sequentially. NOT alphabetical.

  Journal Article:
    [N] Lastname, A. B.; Lastname, C. D. Title of Article. Abbreviated
        Journal Name Year, Volume, Page–Page. DOI: 10.XXXX/XXXXX.

  Book:
    [N] Lastname, A. B. Title of Book, Edition ed.; Publisher: City, Year.

  Book Chapter:
    [N] Lastname, A. B. Chapter Title. In Book Title; Lastname, A. B., Ed.;
        Publisher: City, Year; pp XX–YY.

  Thesis:
    [N] Lastname, A. B. Thesis Title. PhD Dissertation / Master's Thesis,
        University Name, City, Year.

  Website:
    [N] Lastname, A. B. Title. Website Name. URL (accessed Month Day, Year).

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Author names: Lastname, A. B. (Last name + period-separated initials)
  • Multiple authors separated by semicolons
  • Article titles: no italics, sentence case
  • Journal names: abbreviated (e.g., J. Am. Chem. Soc., Angew. Chem.)
  • Minimum 20 references; ALL cited in body text
  • Use real chemistry journal names:
      General Chemistry : J. Am. Chem. Soc., Angew. Chem. Int. Ed.,
                          Chem. Commun., J. Phys. Chem. A/B/C
      Organic Chemistry : Org. Lett., J. Org. Chem., Tetrahedron Lett.
      Analytical        : Anal. Chem., J. Anal. At. Spectrom.
      Materials         : ACS Nano, Chem. Mater., Adv. Mater.
      Biochemistry      : Biochemistry, ACS Chem. Biol., J. Biol. Chem.
