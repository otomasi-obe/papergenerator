================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — APA (American Psychological Association, 7th Edition)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source           :  "...the system is effective (Santoso, 2020)."
  Two authors             :  "...found by Smith & Jones (2022)."
  Three or more authors   :  "...demonstrated (Garcia et al., 2021)."
  Two sources             :  "...shown in multiple studies (Lee, 2019; Kim, 2021)."
  Author name in sentence :  "Smith (2022) argued that..."
  Direct quote            :  "...exact words" (Author, Year, p. XX).
  Rules:
    - Format: (Author, Year) or Author (Year)
    - Separate multiple sources with semicolons, ordered alphabetically
    - "et al." for 3 or more authors
    - No first names — last name only
    - Use "&" inside parentheses; "and" outside

── REFERENCE LIST FORMAT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Alphabetical by first author's last name. Hanging indent.

  Journal Article:
    Lastname, A. B., & Lastname, C. D. (Year). Title of the article in sentence
        case. Journal Name in Title Case, Volume(Issue), Page–Page.
        https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Conference Paper:
    Lastname, A. B. (Year). Title of paper in sentence case. In A. Editor &
        B. Editor (Eds.), Proceedings Title (pp. XX–XX). Publisher.
        https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Book:
    Lastname, A. B. (Year). Title of the book in sentence case (Nth ed.).
        Publisher. https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Book Chapter:
    Lastname, A. B. (Year). Chapter title in sentence case. In C. D. Editor
        (Ed.), Book title in sentence case (pp. XX–XX). Publisher.

  Thesis:
    Lastname, A. B. (Year). Title of dissertation [Doctoral dissertation /
        Master's thesis, University Name]. Database Name.
        https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXXXX

  Website:
    Lastname, A. B. (Year, Month Day). Title of webpage. Organization Name.
        https://www.example.com

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Article titles: sentence case (only capitalize first word and proper nouns)
  • Journal names: Title Case and italicized
  • Volume numbers: italicized
  • Issue numbers: in parentheses, not italicized
  • DOI formatted as: https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXX
  • Minimum 20 references; ALL cited in body text
  • Years: 2010–2025 preferred
  • Use real journal names:
      Psychological journals : Journal of Experimental Psychology, Psychological Science,
                               Developmental Psychology, Journal of Applied Psychology
      Social science journals: Social Forces, American Sociological Review,
                               Journal of Social Issues, Qualitative Research
      Education journals     : Journal of Educational Psychology, Educational Researcher,
                               Teaching and Teacher Education
      Health/medicine        : Journal of Health Psychology, Health Psychology Review,
                               BMC Public Health, American Journal of Public Health

── STRUCTURED REFERENCE OUTPUT (CRITICAL) ─────────────────────────────────────
  References MUST be output as structured JSON objects with full metadata,
  NOT as pre-formatted strings. This allows the system to reformat references
  to ANY citation style automatically.

  Each reference in the "references"."items" array MUST be an object:
  {
    "authors": ["Lastname1, Initials1", "Lastname2, Initials2"],
    "year": 2023,
    "title": "Full title of the article/paper/book",
    "type": "journal|conference|book|book_chapter|thesis|website",
    "journal": "Journal Name (for journal articles)",
    "conference": "Conference Name (for conference papers)",
    "volume": "XX",
    "issue": "Y",
    "pages": "AAA-BBB",
    "doi": "10.XXXX/XXXXXXX",
    "publisher": "Publisher Name (for books/theses)",
    "location": "City, Country (for books/theses)",
    "url": "https://... (for websites)",
    "accessed": "DD Mon YYYY (for websites)",
    "editors": ["Editor Name (for book chapters)"],
    "book_title": "Book Title (for book chapters)",
    "institution": "University Name (for theses)"
  }

  Only include fields relevant to the reference type.
  DO NOT output references as formatted strings like "[1] A. Smith, 'Title'..."
  DO NOT include "_CATATAN" or "_ATURAN_REFERENSI" fields in output.
