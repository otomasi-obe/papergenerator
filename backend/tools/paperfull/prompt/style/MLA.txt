================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — MLA (Modern Language Association, 9th Edition)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source           :  "...the concept is established (Smith 45)."
  Author in sentence      :  "Smith argues that... (45)."
  Two authors             :  "...documented (Smith and Jones 102)."
  Three or more authors   :  "...noted (Garcia et al. 78)."
  Two sources             :  "...shown by multiple scholars (Lee 23; Kim 100)."
  No page number          :  "...discussed (Smith)."
  Rules:
    - Format: (Author PageNumber) — no comma between author and page
    - Use "and" between two authors (not "&")
    - "et al." for four or more authors
    - Separate multiple sources with semicolons
    - No year in the in-text citation

── WORKS CITED FORMAT ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Alphabetical by first author's last name. "Works Cited" as heading.

  Journal Article (print):
    Lastname, Firstname, and Firstname Lastname. "Title of Article in Title
        Case." Journal Name, vol. X, no. Y, Year, pp. AAA–BBB.

  Journal Article (online / DOI):
    Lastname, Firstname. "Title of Article." Journal Name, vol. X, no. Y,
        Year, pp. AAA–BBB. DOI or URL.

  Conference Paper:
    Lastname, Firstname. "Paper Title." Conference Name, Year, City, Country.
        Publisher, Year, pp. XX–YY.

  Book (entire):
    Lastname, Firstname. Title of Book. Edition, Publisher, Year.

  Book Chapter:
    Lastname, Firstname. "Chapter Title." Book Title, edited by Firstname
        Lastname, Publisher, Year, pp. XX–YY.

  Website:
    Lastname, Firstname. "Page Title." Website Name, Day Month Year,
        URL. Accessed Day Month Year.

  Thesis:
    Lastname, Firstname. "Dissertation Title." Year, University Name,
        PhD dissertation / MA thesis.

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Container titles (journals, books, websites): in italics
  • Article and chapter titles: in "double quotation marks"
  • Hanging indent for each entry
  • Minimum 20 works cited; ALL cited in body text
  • Use real humanities/social science journal names:
      Literature    : PMLA, Modern Fiction Studies, American Literature,
                      Journal of Modern Literature
      History       : American Historical Review, Journal of Modern History,
                      Past & Present, History and Theory
      Cultural/Media: Cultural Studies, Media, Culture & Society,
                      Screen, Critical Inquiry
      Language      : Language, Journal of Linguistics, Applied Linguistics,
                      Journal of Pragmatics
