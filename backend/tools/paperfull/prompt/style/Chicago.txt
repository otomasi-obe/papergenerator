================================================================================
CITATION STYLE GUIDE — Chicago (17th Edition, Author-Date System)
================================================================================
Use this format for ALL citations in the paper.

── IN-TEXT CITATION FORMAT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Single source           :  "...the concept is established (Santoso 2020)."
  Author in sentence      :  "Santoso (2020) argues that..."
  Two authors             :  "...shown by (Smith and Jones 2022)."
  Three or more authors   :  "...noted in (Garcia et al. 2021)."
  Two sources             :  "...multiple studies (Lee 2019; Kim 2021)."
  With page number        :  "...as discussed (Smith 2020, 45)."
  Rules:
    - Format: (Author Year) — no comma between author and year
    - Use "and" between two author names
    - "et al." for four or more authors
    - Separate multiple sources with semicolons
    - Add comma + page number for specific quotes: (Smith 2020, 145)

── REFERENCE LIST FORMAT ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Title: "Bibliography" or "References". Alphabetical by last name. Hanging indent.

  Journal Article:
    Lastname, Firstname, and Firstname Lastname. Year. "Title of Article in
        Title Case." Journal Name Volume (Issue): Page–Page.
        https://doi.org/10.XXXX/XXXXXXX.

  Conference Paper:
    Lastname, Firstname. Year. "Paper Title." Paper presented at Conference
        Name, City, Country, Month Date–Date.

  Book (entire):
    Lastname, Firstname. Year. Title of Book. City: Publisher.

  Book Chapter:
    Lastname, Firstname. Year. "Chapter Title." In Book Title, edited by
        Firstname Lastname, XX–XX. City: Publisher.

  Thesis:
    Lastname, Firstname. Year. "Dissertation Title." PhD dissertation /
        Master's thesis, University Name.

  Website:
    Lastname, Firstname. Year. "Page Title." Organization. Accessed Month
        Day. URL.

── FORMATTING RULES ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  • Titles of journals, books: in italics (or underline)
  • Article and chapter titles: in "quotation marks"
  • Full first names (not initials) in the bibliography
  • Year placed immediately after author name in bibliography
  • Minimum 20 references; ALL cited in body text
  • Use real social science, history, and humanities journals:
      Social Sciences  : American Journal of Sociology, Social Forces,
                         Annual Review of Sociology, Theory and Society
      History          : Journal of American History, Past & Present,
                         American Historical Review, Journal of Modern History
      Economics        : American Economic Review, Journal of Political Economy,
                         Quarterly Journal of Economics
      Political Science: American Political Science Review, Comparative Politics,
                         World Politics, Journal of Democracy
